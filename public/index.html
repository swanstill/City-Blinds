Meet Our Team

We're a friendly, experienced team dedicated to providing the best service and quality blinds for your home.

From the first phone call to the final fitting, you'll deal with the same friendly faces every step of the way. We measure your windows ourselves, make sure every blind is made to measure, and install it with care — leaving your room clean and tidy when we leave.

Between us, we've fitted thousands of blinds across homes and businesses in Peterborough and the surrounding area. Whether you need a single blackout blind for the nursery or a full house of roman, roller and vertical blinds, we'll help you choose the right style, fabric and finish to suit your room and your budget.

No call centres, no pushy salespeople — just honest advice, quality products and a team that takes pride in a job well done. If you're not happy, we're not happy, and we'll always go the extra mile to put things right.
